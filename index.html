Privacy

No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising. Your fleet, tracked items and settings live on your device only. The two optional exceptions — an anonymous push token if you enable notifications, and anonymous price reports if you send one — are covered in the full privacy policy.

Where the data comes from

Everything the app shows is drawn from publicly available sources:

  • Roberts Space Industries — ship specifications, pledge store listings, patch notes, comm-links and dev tracker posts.
  • Star Citizen Wiki — component statistics, loadouts, crafting and commodity data. Available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Game data changes with every patch and is gathered automatically, so figures can lag behind the live build. Nothing here should be treated as authoritative — check in game before committing real money to a run.

Known limits

  • Some vehicles and components have no artwork because none is published upstream.
  • A few hulls have no loadout data for the same reason.
  • Prices reflect the most recent successful reading, not a live feed.
