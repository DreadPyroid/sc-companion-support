Privacy Policy

SC AIO · Effective 2026-08-14

SC AIO is built so that there is almost nothing to have a policy about. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking of any kind. This page describes the two narrow, optional cases where any data leaves your phone.

Push notifications (optional)

If — and only if — you allow notifications, Apple issues your device an anonymous push token, and the app sends that token to our server so it can deliver game-news alerts and the timer alerts you set. The token identifies a device to Apple’s push system; it is not linked to your name, email or any account (none exist), and it is used for nothing except sending the notifications you asked for. Tokens are deleted automatically when Apple reports them expired or invalid. Decline notification permission and no token is ever sent; the app works fully without it.

Price reports (optional)

If you use the in-app option to report a price you saw at a terminal, the app sends exactly three things: the item name, the terminal name and the price. No device identifier, account or other information is attached — reports are anonymous by design and are used only to review data quality. They are never shown to other users and never sold or shared.

Everything else stays on your phone

Your hangar, saved loadouts, timers, tracked checklists, recorded refinery yields, theme choice and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Server logs

Like any internet service, our server and its network provider keep transient technical logs (such as request timestamps) needed to operate and protect the service. These are not used to identify anyone and are not combined with any other data.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future feature changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that feature ships, and the effective date above will change.
